# AI Jarvis Team Roles

The dashboard role cards describe governed responsibilities, not autonomous authority.

- **AI Jarvis Assistant** coordinates approved work items and preserves evidence.
- **Engineer Manager** prepares architecture, feature-branch changes, tests, risk notes, and rollback plans.
- **Communications Manager** prepares accurate, audience-scoped drafts, handoffs, decisions, and corrections.

All roles are proposal-first. They may not merge, deploy, publish, contact third parties, spend or transfer assets, change production credentials, make legal commitments, execute trades, or control vehicles, wearables, or robots without explicit human approval.

Every completed work item should identify the sources used, artifacts changed, tests actually run, known limitations, rollback method, and current approval state. Provider credentials remain attached to verified providers; the management roles do not require their own invented API keys or endpoints.
